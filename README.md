# Electric Vehicle Market Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes electric vehicle market data using Tableau. It explores vehicle categories, manufacturer sales, customer ratings, battery technologies, driving range, pricing, and annual sales trends.

## Objectives
- Analyze the distribution of electric vehicle categories.
- Compare annual sales across manufacturers.
- Explore battery technology representation.
- Examine customer ratings across vehicle categories.
- Investigate the relationship between vehicle price and driving range.
- Visualize manufacturing-country distribution and annual sales trends.

## Tools and Technologies
- Tableau Public
- CSV dataset
- Data visualization and exploratory data analysis

## Dashboard Highlights
- EV market KPI overview
- EV category distribution
- Manufacturer sales comparison
- Customer rating analysis
- Annual sales trend
- Battery technology market share
- Manufacturing-country map
- Price vs. driving range scatter plot
- Category vs. battery technology heatmap

## Key Findings
- Two-Wheeler Scooters have the highest vehicle record count in the analyzed dataset.
- AmpereWorks leads the displayed manufacturer sales comparison.
- NMC and LFP account for the largest shares in the battery technology chart.
- Average customer ratings are approximately 4.09 across categories.
